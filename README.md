# 🏢 SB Business Services

SB Business Services was the **parent company of Sweet Bea**, offering **web development and branding solutions** for businesses and startups. The company specialized in **website development, business card design, menu card templates, and brand identity creation** to help businesses establish a strong presence.

Though the business is now **closed**, this repository serves as an **archive** of its contributions.

## 📌 Services Offered
- **Website Development** – Creating responsive and professional websites
- **Business Card Design** – Custom branding solutions
- **Menu Card Templates** – Visual and structured menu designs
- **Brand Identity** – Logos and other branding assets

## 🚀 Technologies Used
- **HTML & CSS** – Structuring and styling web pages
- **Graphic Design** – Business cards, menu cards, and branding elements

This repository preserves the **work and creative contributions** of SB Business Services as a reference for **past projects**.
